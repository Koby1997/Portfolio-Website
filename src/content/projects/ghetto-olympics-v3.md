<h3 class="text-4xl font-bold text-white mb-4">Slingshot is Back!</h3>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
What flies further and more accurately than a spitball? A tennis ball! Now there is a target to hit, but that is not all. 
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
In this team game, athletes must put on XXXXL sweatpants and a t-shirt that are soaked in cold water, and shoot tennis balls at a target. Teammates must field missed balls and throw them back to an area only the shooter can be in. There are only 3 balls per team, so make each shot count!
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
Watching the shooter have to retrieve tennis balls in such oversized wet clothing was so much funnier than I would have imagined.
</p>

<h3 class="text-4xl font-bold text-white">Powderpuff Dodgeball</h3>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
Pretty simple here, just play dodgeball! The only difference is the balls have holes carved out of them and are filled with powdered sugar so when they hit someone, they get covered in a puff of powder!
</p>

<!-- Narrative 3: Text Left, Media Right -->
<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 gap-12 items-center mb-32">
<div class="md:col-span-6 flex flex-col gap-6 text-left">

<h3 class="text-4xl font-bold text-white">Don't Spill the Water</h3>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
In this game, teams were tied together and each given a cup. On one side of a field was a bucket of water, and on the other side was an empty bucket with a line in the middle. The team that could fill their bucket up to the line first won!
Do you go fast and risk spilling water and having to do more trips? Or do you go slow and make every drop count??
</p>
