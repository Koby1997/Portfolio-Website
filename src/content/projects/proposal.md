<p class="text-zinc-300 text-2xl leading-relaxed font-light mb-6">Now this page is going to be way more detailed than the rest of the project, milestone, and event pages.</p>
<p class="text-zinc-300 text-2xl leading-relaxed font-light">There were so many moving parts to this and things to balance and I want to show it all. Now I can't give every single detail, but I will give as much as I can. Now where to start? How about, the first night of testing.</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 gap-12 items-center mb-32">

<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
I knew I wanted to propose to Erin, so the question became how to do it? The thought of building giant letters came to mind, so I decided to see what it would take to do that.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
With some cardboard boxes, tape, and some paint, I was able to make an "M". Now one question comes to mind when you want to make giant letters: "Where am I going to put them?"
</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 gap-12 items-start mb-32">

<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
Luckily I knew a special spot. Jumping back a couple of months, I had surprised Erin with what we called "The Mountain Top Date". I had some friends help me set up a romantic dinner that looked over Colorado Springs.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
Funny enough, this wasn't the original spot. There was a larger open area that was lower on the mountain, but that night, there were other people parked there so we had to find a new spot. This one ended up being higher, more secluded, and perfect for the actual proposal later on.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
This dinner was the first time I told her that I loved her and it became a special place for us.
</p>
</div>
<img src="/assets/projects/proposal/MountainTopDate.jpg" class="w-full h-auto rounded-2xl shadow-2xl border border-zinc-800 object-cover max-h-[500px]" />

<div class="w-16 h-1 bg-cyan-500"></div>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
Now our special spot could actually see the original spot quite easily, and the area was way larger. The perfect spot to place giant letters.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
For reference in the picture, the "M" is leaning against my Honda CRV which you can barely see!
</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 gap-12 items-start mb-32">
<div class="md:col-span-6 flex justify-center items-start order-2 md:order-1">

<div>
<div class="w-16 h-1 bg-cyan-500 mb-6"></div>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
Now seeing how good just that "M" looked, I knew I could make it work. The plan was to simply make 'MARRY ME?'. Now, from testing, I knew with the wind and the size of the letters, I couldn't just have some small supports for cardboard.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
I decided I would make frames for the letters out of wood and then cover them in cardboard. I am not made of money, so I could not just go to Home Depot and buy all of the resources needed.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
For weeks I was able to find Facebook Marketplace posts of people getting rid of wood for free. My friends' work was also getting rid of a ton of cardboard boxes.
</p>
</div>
<img src="/assets/projects/proposal/FreeWood.jpg" class="w-full h-auto rounded-2xl shadow-2xl border border-zinc-800 object-cover max-h-[500px]" />
</div>

<!-- Narrative 5: Text Left, Media Right -->
<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 gap-12 items-center mb-32">
<div class="md:col-span-6 flex flex-col gap-6 text-left">
<div class="w-16 h-1 bg-cyan-500"></div>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
Onto building the actual letters! I would not be able to transport the letters if they were one solid piece, so I had to come up with a way to make them in smaller pieces that could be put together.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
I planned out each letter in advance to make as many rectangular pieces as possible to minimize cuts, store efficiently, and so I could stack them on my car for transporting.
</p>
</div>
<div class="md:col-span-6 flex justify-center">
<img src="/assets/projects/proposal/LettersBlueprint.jpg" class="w-full h-auto rounded-2xl shadow-2xl border border-zinc-800 object-cover max-h-[500px]" />
</div>

<div class="w-16 h-1 bg-cyan-500"></div>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
If I am going to build giant letters, how am I going to hide them from Erin? Luckily I have awesome friends and one of them let me basically take over his garage for months.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
I was able to go over anytime to work in the driveway, then store everything in his garage.
</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 gap-12 items-center mb-32">

<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
Yes, I said months. Luckily I started this project plenty of time in advance because it took way longer than I thought and I had a deadline to make.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
One of the main bottlenecks of this project was that I had 1 saw, 2 drills, and 3 batteries.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
A lot of the wood I got was large planks that needed to be cut down into smaller pieces. Well, it would take pretty much all 3 batteries to just to cut enough wood to make one letter.
</p>
</div>
<div class="md:col-span-6 flex justify-center">

<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
I had to start planning and being strategic with my time whenever I went over to the house to work. I needed to juggle how many batteries to use to cut wood, what batteries can be used to build, how to always have a battery charging, and having enough frames ready to staple cardboard then paint.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
My friends were also a huge help with anything I needed while building the letters. Whether it was stapling cardboard, painting, or cleaning up.
</p>

<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
Now that some real letters were built, I needed to do one final test. I loaded some on my car and drove out to the spot. Would they work? Were they big enough?
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed mb-6">
One thing I haven't mentioned is that I would have to drive past the spot the letters would be in order to get to the spot I would propose. So I also needed to see if you would notice the letters flat on the ground while driving by.
</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
The plan would be to have my friends stand the letters up once our car was out of sight.

</p>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
Now, for a quick side note, I need to explain my plan to still make this a surprise. You see, I can't just tell my girlfriend to get her nails done, dress up nice, and come with me to the place I told her I loved her without her figuring out what was going on.
