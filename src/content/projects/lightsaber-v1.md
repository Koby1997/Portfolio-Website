<h3 class="text-4xl font-bold text-white">The Build</h3>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
The 'Brain' of the saber is a Nano Biscotte V4.0 soundboard. It allowed for multiple colors, default humming sounds, motion detection, and collision detection. I also made it so a random Darth Maul quote plays when the saber is turned on. At this point in time, there weren't really any instruction guides or kits, so I had to research parts, read manuals, and figure out circuitry myself.
</p>
</div>

<h3 class="text-4xl font-bold text-white">Final Look</h3>
<p class="text-zinc-400 text-lg leading-relaxed">
After much debugging from bad soldering and a rushed paint job, it actually turned out great! The saber had real weight to it and the motion/collision detection was very smooth.
</p>
